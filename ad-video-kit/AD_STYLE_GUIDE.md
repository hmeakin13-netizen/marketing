# Apex ad style guide (for homeowner lead ads, including pay-per-lead)

This is how we write and run the Meta ads that get homeowner enquiries for trades (roofers, tree surgeons, landscapers). It works the same whether the leads go to one client on a retainer or are sold per lead.

## 1. What works (from our own numbers, Nov 2026)

- **Real-job video beats images on every client.**
  - Sovereign: the video got all of the leads; the image ads got 0.
  - Efficient: video £7.78 to £9.45 a lead; images £47 or more, and some got none.
  - Eco Ground: video £7.92 to £11 a lead; images £22 to £31.
- **The winning format:** a 20 to 35 second vertical video (9:16) made from the client's own job photos and clips. It has:
  1. a map of the area they cover in the first 3 seconds
  2. big burned-in captions, 2 to 3 words at a time, with key words in yellow
  3. a British male voiceover
  4. a cut every 2 to 3 seconds
- **Facebook beats Instagram for these audiences.** Instagram cost us about 2x per lead. Run on Facebook only: feed, Stories and Reels.
- **Retargeting is the cheapest source.** Show ads again to people who opened the form but didn't send it (90 days). It gets leads at 50p to £3. Always run one, and make sure every new form is added to the retarget audience.
- **Fewer ads per ad set.** 3 to 5 ads, one ad set per audience, and at least about £10 a day per ad set. Lots of ads at £2 to £5 a day each never learn.
- **Don't touch it for 3 days after a change.** Every edit (creative, form, targeting, placement) sends ads back to review and restarts learning. Make all your changes at once, then leave it.
- **Late November (Black Friday) costs about 40% more to reach people.** Don't judge new tests that week, and consider cutting spend on Black Friday itself.

## 2. Rules (never break these)

1. **Real photos and real footage only.** No AI images, AI video or stock footage pretending to be the client's work.
2. **No prices** in ads.
3. **No made-up claims.** Every number, review or result must be real and checkable. "Example" lead screens must say "Example notifications".
4. **No absolute words:** never, every, all, nobody, always, guaranteed (unless it's a real written guarantee).
5. **Always name the area** ("Kent", "North London", "Hereford and the West Midlands"). Local beats generic.
6. **Keep the SMS and email opt-out** in every follow-up. It's a legal requirement.
7. **Pay-per-lead extra rule:** if a lead may go to more than one business, or to a business other than the one named in the ad, the ad and the form must not say "yours only", and the form's privacy text must clearly say who receives the details. UK GDPR requires this.

## 3. Ad structure (homeowner-facing)

**Hook (first line and first 3 seconds of video).** Call out the place and the problem. For example:
- "Kent. Roof leaking or past its best? Watch this first."
- "North London. Flat roof worn out? Read this before winter."
- "Hereford. Garden needs sorting before spring?"

**Body (3 to 5 short lines):**
1. The problem in the homeowner's words: leaks, overgrown trees, a tired patio.
2. What the business does, shown with a real job (before and after works best).
3. One or two trust points that are true: years trading, local team, free survey or quote, insured, written guarantee.
4. Low-pressure call to action: "Tap below for a free, no-obligation quote. Takes 30 seconds."

**Headline:** short and local, for example "Free Roof Check in Kent" or "Free Patio Quote, West Midlands".

**Button:** Get Quote (best), or Get Offer.

**Instant form (keep it short, 3 to 5 questions):**
1. What do you need? (multiple choice of the main jobs)
2. Postcode
3. When do you want it done? (ASAP / within a month / just pricing up)
4. Name, phone and email, pre-filled by Meta

Turn on the "higher intent" review screen. It cuts junk leads.

**Follow-up:** an instant text to the lead from a UK mobile number (07), so it can send texts. Call within 5 minutes during working hours.

## 4. Video build recipe

1. Collect 10 to 20 of the client's own job photos and clips, plus their service area.
2. Script 60 to 85 words, in this order:
   - area call-out plus problem
   - what can go wrong / why act now
   - who they are and what they do, with real proof
   - free quote CTA
3. Voiceover: British male, calm and confident.
4. Visuals:
   - area map first
   - real job shots matched to what the voice is saying
   - text and icons dropping in on the spoken words
   - captions in the lower third
5. End card: business name, area, "Free quote, tap below".
6. Export 1080x1920, 20 to 35 seconds, captions burned in.

## 5. Campaign setup

| Setting | What to use |
|---|---|
| Objective | Leads, instant form (on Facebook) |
| Budget | One prospecting ad set at about £15 to £25 a day per area, plus one retarget ad set (form openers, 90 days) at about £5 to £10 |
| Targeting | Radius around the client's area. Advantage+ audience on, age 25 to 65+ (homeowners) |
| Placements | Facebook feed, Stories, Reels only. No Audience Network, Messenger or Marketplace |
| Ads | 1 video plus 1 or 2 variants, one change between them (hook or first 3 seconds) |
| Judge after | 3 to 5 days, or about £50 spent per ad. Keep anything under target cost per lead; pause anything at 2x target with no leads |

## 6. Prompt to give another chat or AI

> You are writing Meta (Facebook) lead ads for a UK home-improvement trade. Follow these rules strictly:
> - Real photos and footage only. Never suggest AI images or stock footage of "our work".
> - No prices. No made-up claims, reviews or results. Only use facts I give you.
> - No absolute words (never, every, all, nobody, always, guaranteed unless I confirm a written guarantee).
> - Plain UK English, short lines, friendly and direct, written for homeowners aged 30 to 70.
> - Always call out the local area in the first line.
> - If the leads may be sold to or shared with more than one business, do not say "yours only" or similar, and include a privacy line saying who will receive their details.
>
> Inputs I'll give you: trade, area/towns covered, main jobs, true trust points (years trading, insured, guarantees, reviews), the offer (e.g. free quote/survey), and whether leads are exclusive or shared.
>
> Produce:
> 1. 3 hooks (first line plus first 3 seconds of video), each naming the area and a problem.
> 2. 2 complete ad texts (60 to 110 words each): hook, problem, real proof, free quote CTA.
> 3. 3 headlines under 40 characters, local.
> 4. A 25 to 35 second video script (60 to 85 words) for a British male voiceover, with a shot list matched line by line to real job footage. Start with an area map, and say which words should pop up on screen.
> 5. A 4-question instant form (job type, postcode, timeframe, contact details) and the privacy line to use.
> 6. One thing to A/B test first and why.
>
> Before writing, list any facts you are missing and ask for them. Don't invent them.
